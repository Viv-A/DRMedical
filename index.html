Flexio

Remote patients need remote physiotherapy.


Flexio is a web and mobile application to remotely monitor physiotherapy compliance and help patients achieve a successful recovery post injury or operation.


With Flexio, you can:


Create personalised treatments for each patient and record the exercises with the accompanying app.

The Flexio app records real-time data for analysis of patient compliance during rehabilitaion.
Using the Flexio dashboard, you can easly identify patients who are non-compliant.
Achieve better results through early intervention without the requirement for frequent hospital visits.
Feedback through the app gives patients confidence in their ability to perform the exercises and recover.

D+R Balance

The D+R Balance application uses smartphone sensor technology to more accurately assess the extent of subject rotation during the Unterberger manoeuvre, or Fukuda step test.


The phone can be held by the subject or attached to the arm during the recording.


The application also records body sway, for example Romberg's or Tandem Romberg test. sway is measured over a 30 second period to determine an accurate result.


Learn More...

Values are presented as follows:

Xm, Ym, Zm - X, Y, Z axis mean

Xd, Yd, Zd - 2 standard deviations from the X, Y, Z mean value

K - the Kanegaonkar value, the volume of the ellipse that encloses Xd, Yd and Zd.

D+R Hearing

D+R Hearing is a bluetooth connected digital tuning fork.

With the accompanying smart phone app, doctors can reliably choose from 4 frequencies and 4 amplitudes to test patient bone conduction.

Covid 19

As a result of our desire to help rehabiliate patients, we designed a 3D printed* breathing device for Covid 19 recovery.


With the addition of a ping pong ball, patients with breathing impairment can improve resporatory function by exhaling through the device and keeping the ball floating as long as possible.


*By downloading this model you accept full responsibilty for its use. The print takes 30 hours and needs the supporting material thoroughly removed before use.

DO NOT inhale through the device.
